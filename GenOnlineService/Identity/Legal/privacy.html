1. Who is responsible

The Generals Online team, in the Netherlands, is responsible for the personal data described here (the controller under the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). For any question about your data or to use your rights, email support@generals.online.

2. What we store and why

Your account

  • Your email address and whether you confirmed it, to sign you in, to confirm your address and to reset your password.
  • Your display name and tag, and the names you used before, so that others can find you and so that staff can recognise renamed players.
  • Your password, only as a one-way hash, never the password itself. Accounts made with a sign-in provider have no password.
  • Two-factor sign-in, when you turn it on: the authenticator key, stored encrypted, and your recovery codes, stored only as hashes.
  • The state of your account (active or blocked), failed sign-in attempts and temporary lockouts, when the account was created and when you last signed in.
  • Your settings, friends, friend requests and blocked players.

Sign-in providers

When you sign in with Discord, Steam, Google or GameReplays, we store which provider it is, the provider's id for your account (your Discord user id, SteamID64, Google account id or GameReplays member id), when you linked it and when you last used it. We do not store your name, picture or email address from the provider. The name a provider gives us is only offered as a suggestion when you choose your display name. When a provider shares your email address, we only compare it with existing accounts to link your account, and do not keep it.

Connection and device data

  • Your IP address. From it we work out your country with a GeoLite2 database (MaxMind) that runs on our own server, so your address is not sent anywhere for this.
  • Each time the game connects, we record the IP address, the country, the game client and the time. We keep these records for 90 days.
  • The game also sends identifiers of your computer: the Windows machine id, the network adapter's MAC address and the disk's volume serial number. We store them with the last IP address they were seen from, and keep each one for 180 days after it was last seen.
  • Your continent and approximate coordinates, also worked out from your IP address, are used by lobbies and matchmaking. They are kept in memory while you are online and are not stored in the database.

We use this data to keep the service secure, to enforce bans and to find accounts used to evade them. Staff can see it when they investigate a player, and every such view is recorded.

Chat

We store the chat messages you send in channels, lobbies, direct messages and groups, with who sent them, to whom and when, so that they can be delivered and so that reports can be dealt with. Messages are deleted 30 days after they were sent; a job removes them every hour.

Reports, sanctions and the staff log

  • A report stores who reported whom, why, and the evidence: the reported message with up to 10 messages before and after it, or the reported player's last 10 messages that the reporter could see, including direct messages to the reporter. A report is kept until staff close it, and deleted 30 days after that.
  • Sanctions (kicks, mutes and bans) store the reason, who gave them, when, and when they end or were lifted.
  • Every staff action, such as a sanction, a name reset, viewing a report or looking up a message, is recorded in a staff log with its reason.

We keep sanctions and the staff log without a fixed end, so that repeated abuse can be recognised and staff are accountable for what they do.

Agreements

We record which version of the Rules of Engagement and the Terms of Service you agreed to, and when.

Matches and statistics

We store your matches: the lobby settings, the map, the players with their teams, sides, colours and results, your rating and your statistics, and the leaderboards. Match data is part of the public record of the game and is kept without a fixed end.

The game can upload replays and screenshots of matches; this is switched off at the moment. When it is on, they are stored in the European Union and deleted after 90 days. Screenshots taken by the anti-cheat are kept apart and for as long as a review or a sanction needs them, since they can be the evidence for a ban.

Logs and error reports

  • Our web server logs each request with the IP address, the address requested, the time, the browser's user agent and the referring page. These logs are kept for 14 days and are checked automatically for attacks.
  • The service writes its own logs of what it does and of errors.
  • When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends an error report to our own error tracker (GlitchTip, at errors.generals.online), which runs on our servers, so that we can fix it. A report holds technical details of the error and of your computer, and can include the IP address it came from. Error reports are deleted after 90 days.

Backups

We back up the database, encrypted, every day, to Cloudflare R2 storage held in the European Union. Backups are kept for about four weeks and then replaced.

Children

Generals Online is for players aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly keep personal data of anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we close it and delete its personal data, except what we must keep to deal with a report or a ban.

3. Our legal grounds

  • Contract: to run your account and the service you signed up for: signing in, chat, lobbies, matches, ratings, and the emails that confirm your address or reset your password.
  • Legitimate interest: to keep the service secure and fair: connection and device data, logs, error reports, backups, reports, sanctions and the staff log. We keep this to what is needed and for the times stated above.
  • Consent: when you choose to sign in with a provider such as Discord, Steam, Google or GameReplays. You can stop using it at any time.

4. Who else receives data

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising. Some data reaches these services, which work for us or which you choose to use:

  • Brevo sends our emails (address confirmation and password reset) from the generals.online domain. It receives your email address and the email.
  • Discord, Steam, Google and GameReplays, when you choose to sign in with them. They learn that you signed in to Generals Online.
  • Discord, when the team turns on the copy of public channel chat: a message from a chat channel is posted to a Discord channel of the Generals Online team, with the room number, your display name, your user id and the text. Lobby, direct and group messages are never copied.
  • GameReplays (Strata leaderboards): the results of matches, with your user id, display name, side, team, colour and match statistics, so that they appear on its leaderboards.
  • Epic Online Services: the game receives a short-lived token naming your player id, which Epic checks to connect the game to its online services.
  • Cloudflare relays the game's network traffic between players when a direct connection is not possible. It then sees your IP address.
  • Cloudflare stores our encrypted database backups in R2, with the data kept in the European Union.
  • Our hosting providers run the servers that hold the data: netcup in Germany, OVHcloud in France, Germany and the United Kingdom, and Worldstream in the Netherlands. The United Kingdom is outside the European Economic Area; the European Commission has decided that it protects personal data adequately, so data can go there without further safeguards.

5. Cookies

The account pages use only the cookies they need to work:

  • go_session keeps you signed in, for up to 14 days.
  • go_two_factor remembers, for 5 minutes, that you still have to enter your two-factor code.
  • go_antiforgery protects the forms against requests forged by other sites.
  • go_culture remembers the language you chose, for 1 year.
  • While you sign in with Discord, Steam, Google or GameReplays, a short-lived cookie links the provider's answer to your sign-in.

We use no analytics, tracking or advertising cookies, and the pages load nothing from other sites.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectification of data that is wrong (you can change your display name yourself on your account page);
  • erasure of your data, unless we must keep it, for example to enforce a ban;
  • restriction of how we use your data;
  • portability: a copy of the data you gave us, in a common format;
  • objection to the use of your data based on our legitimate interest.

Email support@generals.online from the email address of your account, or tell us which sign-in provider you use, so that we can check it is you. We answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

7. Changes

When we change what we store or why, we update this policy. When the change affects the Terms of Service, you are asked to agree to the new Terms at your next sign-in.
