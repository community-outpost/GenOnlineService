These rules apply everywhere on Generals Online: in chat, in lobbies and matches, in display names and clan tags, and on the account pages. You agree to them when you first sign in, one card at a time. This page explains each card in more detail.

1. Respect

Treat every player with respect, whether they are your teammate, your opponent or new to the game.

  • No racism, hate or discrimination. Do not attack anyone for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any similar trait.
  • No politics or religion. Leave political and religious debates out of chat. They split the community and rarely stay civil.
  • No harassment. Do not insult, bully, stalk or keep messaging a player who does not want to hear from you.
  • No threats. Do not threaten anyone with harm, in or out of the game, even as a joke.
  • No doxxing. Do not share anyone's personal information, such as a real name, address, photo, contact details or workplace, without their permission.

2. Chat, names and clan tags

Keep chat readable and your name fit for everyone to see.

  • No spamming. Do not flood chat with repeated, meaningless or all-caps messages, or keep posting the same link or request.
  • No disruptive behaviour. Do not derail conversations, bait other players into fights or make chat unusable for others.
  • No offensive names or clan tags. Your display name and clan tag are seen by everyone. Staff can reset a name that breaks these rules, and you then choose a new one.

3. Fair play

Win with your own skill.

  • No cheating. Do not use hacks, modified game files, map revealers or any tool that gives you an unfair advantage.
  • Accounts and sanctions. You may hold more than one account. Each account is a separate player with its own name, friends and statistics. A sanction applies to every account you hold, and using a new account to avoid a sanction is itself a violation.
  • No win trading or boosting. Do not arrange results with other players, and do not play on someone else's account to raise their rating.
  • No throwing. Do not lose on purpose, whether to lower your own rating or to let someone else win.

4. Match behaviour

Play the match through.

  • No quitting on purpose. Do not leave or disconnect to avoid a loss or to spoil the match for others. If you must go, say so and surrender.
  • No bug or disconnect abuse. Do not use game bugs, forced disconnects or desyncs to win or to stop a match from counting.
  • Respect the host and lobby rules. The host chooses the map and the settings. If you do not like them, find another lobby.

5. Accounts

Your account is yours, and you are responsible for what happens on it.

  • No sharing accounts. Do not let anyone else play on your account, and do not play on someone else's.
  • No impersonation. Do not pretend to be another player, a member of staff or the Generals Online team.
  • No ban evasion. Do not create or use another account while you are banned or muted. Staff can see when accounts share a device or a connection.

6. The service

Generals Online is run by volunteers. Help us keep it running.

  • No attacks on the service. Do not overload, probe, flood or try to break into the servers, and do not automate accounts or the game against the service.
  • Found a security problem? Tell us privately. Email security@generals.online. Do not use the problem, and do not share it publicly before it is fixed.

When rules are broken

You can report a chat message or a player from the game. A report keeps the reported message with the messages around it, or the player's recent messages that you could see, so that staff can judge it in context.

Staff decide on reports and can act on what they see themselves. Depending on how serious the breach is, they can:

  • kick you, which disconnects you from the service;
  • mute you, which stops you from chatting for a set time;
  • ban you, for a set time or permanently;
  • reset your display name.

Every action by staff is recorded with its reason. If you think a decision was wrong, email support@generals.online.
