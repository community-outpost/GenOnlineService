Generals Online is a free, fan-run community service for Command & Conquer Generals Zero Hour. It provides accounts, chat, lobbies, matchmaking, ratings and leaderboards for the game.

3. Your account

You can create an account with an email address and a password, or sign in with Discord, Steam, Google or GameReplays when those are offered. You choose a display name, which is shown with a number tag (Name#1234).

  • Use one account for yourself. Playing on a second account to face weaker opponents is smurfing, which the rules forbid.
  • Keep your password and your sign-in providers safe. You can turn on two-factor sign-in on your account page.
  • You are responsible for what happens on your account. Do not share it with anyone.
  • If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and tell us.

You must be 16 or older to create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we close it.

4. How you must behave

You must follow the Rules of Engagement. They cover respect, chat, names and clan tags, fair play, match behaviour, accounts and the service itself. You agree to them, and to these terms, before you play.

5. Moderation and sanctions

Volunteer staff keep the service fair and safe. When you break the rules or these terms, staff can:

  • kick you, which disconnects you from the service;
  • mute you for a set time, so you cannot chat;
  • ban you for a set time or permanently;
  • reset your display name so you have to choose a new one.

Staff record every action with its reason. If you disagree with a decision, email support@generals.online and tell us why.

6. Your chat messages

You are responsible for what you write. We store chat messages so that they can be delivered and so that staff can deal with reports. We delete them 30 days after they were sent.

  • When someone reports a message or a player, the report keeps the messages involved, and staff read them to decide.
  • When staff investigate a problem, they can look up a single message, including a direct message. Each lookup is recorded with its reason.
  • Messages in the public chat channels may be copied to a Discord channel run by the Generals Online team.

The Privacy Policy explains what we store and for how long.
